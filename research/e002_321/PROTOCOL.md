# E002-321 prospective measurement protocol

1. Signal: exposure-mean normalized eyelid closure; reference: synchronized high-frame-rate eye recording.
2. Prior art: classical Lipschitz optimal recovery, high-speed eyelid kinematics, exposure-integration limits.
3. Metric: certified threshold absence and maximum hidden time above threshold.
4. Confound: geometric and photometric calibration drift.
5. Model: RGB image integrates a changing scene over each exposure; only an independently validated scalar inversion is analyzed.
6. Falsification: a reference crossing above the frozen upper bound, while all calibration assumptions hold.
7. Boundary: M=min(1,b(E,L,eta)+L(P-E)/2).
8. Final evaluation: untouched participant and device, including lighting and eyewear variation. Not yet performed.
9. Publication gate: validated calibration, independent reproduction, four nuisance baselines and prospective crossing prediction.
10. Product rule: report conditional non-detection guarantees only; otherwise abstain.

Controls: geometry-only, brightness-only, missingness-only and temporal-smoothness. No human evaluation occurred. All reported numbers are synthetic.
