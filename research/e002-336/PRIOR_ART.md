# Primary prior art / dataset suitability, 2026-10-21
- Papoutsaki et al., Eye of the Typer / EOTT, ETRA 2018, https://webgazer.cs.brown.edu/data/ : 51 participants, RGB webcam, Tobii Pro X3-120 raw pupil fields, device and eyewear metadata. WebM timestamps and screen sync may be unreliable.
- Shah et al., EyeDentify, WACV 2025, https://github.com/vijulshah/eyedentify : webcam/Tobii pupil pairs, but published reference-folding preprocessing compromises rapid-waveform reference (E002-332).
- Aguirre, Scientific Reports 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-45827-3 : corneal refraction and gaze alter entrance pupil projection.
- Klingner, ETRA 2010, https://doi.org/10.1145/1743666.1743727 : formal pupil-measurement metrology.
- Facemorphic, ECCV Workshops 2024/2025, https://www.micc.unifi.it/resources/datasets/facemorphic/ : synchronized RGB/event cameras but access gated and ~80 GB.
- Clock interpolation, bounded-error identification and sampling theory are established. No novelty claim for this theorem.
