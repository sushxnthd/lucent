# E002-330 — Frozen human-reference protocol (10 mandatory fields)
Date 2026-10-20. This is a preregistration, NOT a human evaluation.

1. SIGNAL/REFERENCE: d=log(z(t)/z(t0)), where z is a geometry-only pupil/limbus radius ratio fitted from ordinary RGB. x=log(D_Tobii(t)/D_Tobii(t0)), synchronized reference pupil diameter. t0=first valid frame per 3-second session. No label-dependent image selection. No contour fit -> MISSING, not imputed. Public EyeDentify 90-Hz labels are averaged to 30-Hz: no rapid waveform inference.
2. LITERATURE: EyeDentify, Shah et al. arXiv:2407.11204; EyeDentify++ arXiv:2408.10397; CondSeg arXiv:2408.17231; group split-conformal prediction is established (Angelopoulos & Bates arXiv:2107.07511).
3. OBSERVABILITY: e=d-x; one participant score S_p=max |e| over all preregistered valid session/frame pairs. Calibrate B as order statistic k=ceil((n_cal+1)(1-alpha)); if k>n_cal then B=infinity. For exchangeable participants, new participant's entire valid sequence has marginal coverage >=1-alpha. Sign certify only d>B or d<-B; equality abstains. This is NOT device-shift conditional coverage.
4. CONFOUND: screen color changes true pupil size AND RGB eye/skin brightness. Gaze, aperture, pupil/iris differential magnification, blur, glasses and blink-removal missingness are additional confounds.
5. GENERATIVE MODEL: I_j(u)=Q_j{integral w_j(t)[G_j(t)*(K_j*Render(u;r,R,g,a,O,S))+B_j(t)]dt+n_j(u)}. Under planar approximation z=(r/R)*m, hence d=x+nu+eps; B is a population calibration bound on residual, not a physical hard bound.
6. FALSIFICATION: declare no observable if two reliable contours unavailable on >=50% of eligible open-eye frames, participant coverage below predeclared nominal target, or sign certification fails versus reference. Compare on same frames with geometry-only eye width/gaze, brightness-only intensity/screen color, missingness-only blink/validity, temporal-smoothness-only interpolation, shuffled-time, and outside-pupil-only controls. Participant bootstrap, not frame bootstrap.
7. FROZEN UNSEEN PREDICTION: after B calibrated but before holdout references are accessed, save per-frame sign predictions: d>B -> dilation; d<-B -> constriction; otherwise abstain. Sharp statistical boundary |d|=B. This is not a new physical invariant.
8. HOLDOUT: SHA256 sort unique participant IDs with fixed seed lucent-e002-330-2026-10-20; first 18 train, next 22 calibrate, final 11 untouched test. Freeze eligibility and timestamp matching before test. EyeDentify has ONE device; device holdout IMPOSSIBLE. Glasses underrepresented; lighting-color stress is exploratory and NOT guaranteed by conformal coverage.
9. PUBLICATION GATE: actual human participant-level coverage, finite nontrivial B, >=25% sign-certification yield, independent checker, nuisance controls, participant CIs, frozen prediction, plus external device and glasses cohort before universal RGB claims. Existing conformal/geometry methods are not claimed novel.
10. PRODUCT: ALLOW only calibrated interval-valued pupil change sign with abstention after human evidence. REJECT uncalibrated pupil waveform, rapid fatigue, five-second fatigue diagnosis, device transfer, and blink timing from blink-pruned data. If 32x16 images cannot support conics, pivot to a separately referenced blink dataset.

ACCESS CAP: max two substantive attempts on this data path. No unauthorized download, commercial reuse, or endless metadata audit.
