# E002-230 | Original-human RGB radial-edge preflight

Active question: does a dimensionless local radial edge in ordinary RGB eye crops remain measurable under photometric and iris-center perturbations?

Signal/reference: RGB angular-median pupil-edge candidate radius divided by iris radius; eventual reference is native-time same-eye Tobii pupil diameter. No physiological pairing is performed in this preflight.

Frontier: E002-229 shows geometry-only iris admission can pass despite unreliable center/ring contrast; original P_33/P_54 source hashes are verified, but exposure-clock mapping remains uncalibrated.

Scalar observability: eventual held-out participant-bootstrap lower Pearson correlation bound. Here exploratory fraction of candidate radial edges surviving brightness/gamma and iris-center perturbations.

Strongest confound: iris pigmentation and texture, partial occlusion, illumination, gaze-induced projected geometry, and center localization.

Falsification: fewer than 75% of 30 development frames per eye satisfy prespecified radial-edge perturbation gates; this kills the candidate detector, not ordinary-RGB physiological observability.

Publishable threshold: >=8 untouched independently synchronized participants, device/lighting/glasses holdouts, independent checker, nuisance controls and mechanism-derived unseen prediction.

Product relevance: >=75% prospective windows accepted under frozen waveform gates; this preflight only screens whether a pupil-edge estimator merits paired evaluation.

Kill condition: two blocked attempts per data path force a switch; three nondiscriminating primary runs force reformulation. No APST-5 or fatigue inference.

SCIENCE: original human iris visibility is insufficient to certify pupil measurement; radial-edge robustness untested until remote execution.
PRODUCT: retain abstention and measurement-only focus; no app work.
