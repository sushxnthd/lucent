# Lucent product consequences

**Reject:** using the inspected three-second time-folded labels to certify rapid pupil waveform, subsecond latency/phase/lag, or fatigue from five-second RGB scans.

**Conditional allowance:** three-second mean pupil-size comparison may survive when equal sample counts and validity weights hold, but still requires calibrated illumination, gaze, and individual baselines.

**Redesign:** require original per-frame camera times, per-sample reference times, exposure windows, independent synchronization and device calibration. Report uncertainty intervals with abstention. Switch to independent synchronized RGB/NIR blink reference if EyeDentify native data remain inaccessible.
