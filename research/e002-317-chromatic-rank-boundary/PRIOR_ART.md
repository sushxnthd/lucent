# Prior art and novelty gate

Classical RGB linear unmixing, nullspace projection, deterministic inverse-problem bounds and metamerism predate E002-317. Do not claim new mathematical priority.

Relevant primary research:
- Susman et al., Comparing Computer Vision Instruments for Eye Blink Analysis, LREC 2026 (tool-specific pose effects).
- Blink parameters are confounded by vertical eye orientation in video-based eye tracking (2026; PubMed 41860661).
- Daza et al., mEBAL2 multispectral eyeblink benchmark, Pattern Recognition Letters (2024), DOI 10.1016/j.patrec.2024.04.011.
- Zhang and Sato, reflective/fluorescent component separation, IEEE TPAMI (2013), DOI 10.1109/TPAMI.2012.255.
- Abeysekera et al., Comprehensive Modeling of Camera Spectral and Color Behavior (2025), arXiv:2507.04617.
- Berns, The proper use of indices of metamerism, Color Research & Application (2008), DOI 10.1002/col.20450.

The scientific opportunity is prospective calibration and falsification of the spectral-angle boundary under ordinary RGB and synchronized high-speed reference, including lighting, gaze, glasses, device and participant holdouts.
