# E002-355 — Gaze and pupil-area observability

Reduced model: with shared affine projection, sqrt(pupil area / iris area) equals the physical radius ratio. With fixed unknown differential plane tilt, apparent ratio can change solely with gaze. The exact conditional sign boundary is in RESULTS.json.

Example: gaze 5 to 40 degrees, tilt bound 5 degrees, log error bound 0.005. Positive change is certified only when apparent log ratio change exceeds 0.03660848. All angles and bounds are illustrative, not human calibrated.

No independent human evaluation. No claim of novel mathematics. See downloadable E002-355 package for complete reproducibility files.
