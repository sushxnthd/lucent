# SCIENCE
Five-second counts do not identify unrestricted long-run blink rates. Under a homogeneous Poisson assumption, unbiased relative rate precision is bounded by 1/sqrt(lambda*T). This assumption has not been validated with participant event timestamps.

# PRODUCT
Retain blink-event counts with uncertainty, not an unqualified five-second long-run rate estimate. Primary pupil-waveform research remains unchanged.
