# SCIENCE → PRODUCT

REJECT uncalibrated RGB claims of no blink, zero closure dose, PERCLOS, rapid pupil-waveform recovery, or five-second fatigue absence.

CALIBRATE exposure duration T, gap G, camera/ISP feature response f, nuisance error eta, and worst-case normalized eyelid speed V against independent synchronized high-speed eye reference. Gaze, head pose, lighting, glasses and device changes must be held out.

ALLOW ONLY AS A RESEARCH TARGET: conditional worst-case upper bounds on *missed anatomical closure dose* when the periodic and calibration assumptions hold. A bound is not a point estimate or diagnosis. Abstain when the upper bound exceeds the decision tolerance.

REDESIGN away from an app-building loop; prefer calibrated observability and explicit abstention. Human calibration is still absent.
