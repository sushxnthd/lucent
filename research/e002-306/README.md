# E002-306: pupil arc observability

Exact conditional fixed-chord circular arc minimax boundary. Independent synthetic verification passed. No human RGB-reference validation; geometric ambiguity is established prior art (Worring & Smeulders 1995, DOI 10.1109/34.387505).

Under edge uncertainty eta, two pupil radii R1,R2 with common chord halfwidth a have indistinguishable full contours exactly when |s(R1,a)-s(R2,a)| <= 2eta, where s(R,a)=R-sqrt(R^2-a^2).

Product: reject uncalibrated pupil radius and five-second fatigue certification; only calibrated interval-valued measurement with abstention is potentially defensible.
