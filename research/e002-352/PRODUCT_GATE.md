# Product gate
ALLOW as research-only: directly observed native-RGB closure-positive frames, quality flags, and calibrated abstention.
CALIBRATE: exposure, clock, eye aperture, geometry, illumination, glasses and independently referenced errors before timing claims.
REJECT: no-blink certification from missing positives, unconditional millisecond duration, peak velocity, rapid pupil waveform, five-second fatigue inference.
REDESIGN: retain native frame timestamps, report phase/missingness vulnerability and separate same-video annotation from independent physiological reference.
