# Prior-art collision: Barry and Wang (2023)

Scientific Reports 13:13841, DOI 10.1038/s41598-023-40796-0. This study measured dynamic pupil responses using RGB smartphone sensors with far-red illumination, a 630-nm long-pass filter, macro lens and eyecup. The abstract reports 451% average pupil/iris contrast improvement and 2.4% mean absolute relative-change error against a clinical instrument. The PDF also reports average correlation 0.93 in its analyzed validation set.

The device is NOT an unmodified webcam. The reference measures the opposite eye, some recordings were manually aligned, and clinical blink labels controlled exclusions. It does not meet Lucent's same-eye, independent-clock, reference-blind protocol.

SCIENCE: RGB-sensor pupillometry already exists with spectral assistance. PRODUCT: preserve an ordinary-webcam tier only if untouched human holdouts pass; otherwise abstain or investigate an optional assisted tier, without new hardware expenditure.
