# E002-369 — Clock gauge and bracketing-anchor observability

Exact conditional affine-clock identified-set theorem and synthetic checker. No human paired validation; no publication-level novelty claim.

Without an independent offset anchor, physiological latency is not identifiable from native RGB/reference streams. With bounded offset, drift and onset, report an interval. Two bracketing anchors can remove growth of drift uncertainty within their interval but cannot restore shutter-lost events.

Full reproducibility package: `lucent_e002_369_clock_gauge.zip` (conversation artifact). All executable files and protocols are intended to be mirrored under this research folder.
