# E002-299: exact shutter-coverage bound

For any c(t) in [0,1] and disjoint exposure windows of total duration fraction rho, the closed-time fraction can range from zero to 1-rho when every exposed interval is open and no temporal regularity is assumed. Therefore a certificate that closed-time fraction is at most p0 requires rho >= 1-p0. At p0=1/5 the transition is rho=4/5. This bound is sharp for the unrestricted signal class; bounded velocity changes it. No empirical validation.
