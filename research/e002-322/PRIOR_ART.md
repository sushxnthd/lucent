# Primary prior-art audit (2026-10-20)

- High-speed camera characterization of voluntary eye blinking kinematics (2013), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4043155/ — 600 fps, reports closure/reopening asymmetry, not hard cross-person bounds.
- Analysis of Spontaneous Eyelid Blink Dynamics Using a 240 Frames per Second Smartphone Camera (2019), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30893187/ — 10-person prospective 240-fps kinematic study.
- The method for quantified analysis and pattern visualization for eye blinking using high-frame-rate video (2025), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12638546/ — 80 participants, reports limitations of 30-fps blink characterization.
- Classical Lipschitz optimal recovery and sampling theory subsume the envelope proof strategy.

Priority search is non-exhaustive; no field-level novelty claim.
