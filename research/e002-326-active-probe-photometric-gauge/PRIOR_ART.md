# Primary prior-art audit
- Mathot 2018, Pupillometry: Psychology, Physiology, and Function, https://doi.org/10.5334/joc.18 — delayed pupil light response and luminance confounding.
- Bernal-Molina et al. 2015, high-resolution binocular video-oculography and PLR, https://doi.org/10.1186/s12938-015-0016-6 — roughly 200 ms PLR latency, not a universal hard lower bound.
- Barry and Wang 2023, RGB smartphone far-red pupillometry with extra optics, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-40796-0 — human validated with filter; does NOT establish unmodified front camera capability.
- Shah et al. 2024, EyeDentify, https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11204 — webcam/Tobii paired but prior preprocessing audit identified reference folding.
- Klingner 2010, remote video eye-tracker metrology, https://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/pupillometric-precision/ — independent calibration necessary.
- Classical bounded-error hypothesis testing, inverse-problem identifiability and sampling theory predate this result.

Novelty gate FAIL: the conditional minimax certificate is not yet a proven field-level discovery. Its value is a falsifiable design rule for active RGB experiments.
