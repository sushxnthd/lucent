# Primary prior-art audit, 2026-10-22

- Navascues-Cornago et al., The Ocular Surface 40 (2026) 22-30, DOI 10.1016/j.jtos.2026.01.003: 500-fps infrared human blink video digitally downsampled to 250/100/50/25 fps; low-fps peak velocities underestimated. Exact exposure resimulation method not independently checked, so do not claim the authors used naive frame decimation.
- Kim et al., Event-guided Deblurring of Unknown Exposure Time Videos, arXiv:2112.06988: frame interval and exposure differ, dynamic exposure matters.
- Çoğalan et al., Computer Graphics Forum (2023), DOI 10.1111/cgf.14748: short and long exposures contain different temporal motion information.
- Culemann et al., Behavior Research Methods (2026), DOI 10.3758/s13428-026-02984-4: human blink measurements confounded by vertical gaze.
- Wasserstein-1 quantile coupling and Kantorovich-Rubinstein duality are established mathematical tools. The E002-351 formula is not independently novel.

Publication novelty gate: FAIL pending independent human RGB results and prospective boundary validation.
