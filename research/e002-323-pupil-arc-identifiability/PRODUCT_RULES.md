# E002-323 Lucent capability gate

ALLOW (research-only): interval-valued, exposure-matched pupil/iris radius when known chord, sagitta error, projective correction, optical magnification, iris scale and timing are independently validated.

ABSTAIN: shat<=epsilon, insufficient visible arc, uncalibrated ISP/blur/illumination, uncontrolled gaze/glasses optics, within-exposure pupil dynamics, unknown clock or segmentation error.

REJECT: uncalibrated rapid pupil waveforms, physiological lag from downsampled reference labels, unrestricted pupil dilation certification, five-second fatigue diagnosis.

PIVOT: calibrated eyelid-closure threshold exclusion remains a better-supported candidate for a human experiment. No app-building loop.
