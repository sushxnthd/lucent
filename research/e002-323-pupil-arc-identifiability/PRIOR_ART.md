# E002-323 primary prior art (20 October 2026)

T. Hopp, The Sensitivity of Three-Point Circle Fitting, NISTIR 5501 (1994), DOI 10.6028/NIST.IR.5501, https://www.nist.gov/publications/sensitivity-three-point-circle-fitting — explicitly establishes circle parameter sensitivity to arc angle. Strong novelty blocker.

Hayes & Petrov, Mapping and correcting the influence of gaze position on pupil size measurements (2016), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4637269/ — gaze-dependent pupil foreshortening.

Petersch & Dierkes, Gaze-angle dependency of pupil-size measurements in head-mounted eye tracking (2022), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9046372/ — perspective, gaze and corneal refraction.

Snip & Stitch: a simple and accessible correction for the pupil foreshortening error (2026), https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-026-02980-8 — recent gaze/obscuration correction work.

Shah et al., EyeDentify, WACV 2025, https://www.dfki.de/en/web/research/projects-and-publications/publication/15080 — paired webcam/reference research, but released temporal label provenance requires native timestamp verification (see E002-319).

Conclusion: arc conditioning and optical confounds are established. This is a conditional engineering observability gate, not a new field-level theorem or validated human result.
