Exact relative dose-interval width under a calibrated power response f(c)=c^p is (1-rho)+rho*(b^(1/p)-a), where rho is shutter duty, a=max(0,y-eta), b=min(1,y+eta), and y is the same observed response mean per exposure. For a predeclared tolerance tau, the sharp acceptance boundary is rho >= (1-tau)/(1-(b^(1/p)-a)). If the required rho exceeds 1, no frame rate or shutter duty can pass the gate without additional waveform assumptions.
