All-time monotone gauge impossibility. Assume observed channels r_j(t)=f_j(c(t)) with unknown strictly increasing responses. For any increasing bijection h:[0,1]->[0,1] fixing endpoints, let c'=h(c), f'_j=f_j composed with h inverse. Then all observed channel values agree at every instant, under any shutter schedule, although integral c can differ from integral c'. Exact example: c=t/W, c'=(t/W)^2, f1(c)=c^2, f2(c)=c^3, f1'(v)=v, f2'(v)=v^(3/2). Anatomical doses are W/2 versus W/3. Endpoint-only calibration is insufficient. This is conditional on an unknown response family, not a theorem about fully calibrated RGB cameras.
