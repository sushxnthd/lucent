For a compact attainable closure set S and known continuous channel responses f1,...,fm, all-time exposure averages y_j=E f_j(c) determine E c for every measurable history iff identity x belongs to span{1,f1,...,fm} on S. Sufficiency: average the affine identity. Necessity: if identity is outside the span, a signed measure annihilating 1,f_j but not x yields two probability laws with equal observations and different E c. Exact rational collision for f1=c^2,f2=c^3 and S={0,1/3,2/3,1}: A=(0,9/10,0,1/10), B=(11/20,0,9/20,0). Both give E c^2=1/5, E c^3=2/15; E c is 2/5 versus 3/10. Repeated every exposure, five-second doses differ by 0.5s despite identical two-channel averages. This does not exclude recovery using other calibrated spatial channels.
