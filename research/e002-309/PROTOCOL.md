# E002-309 research protocol

Signal: exposure-integrated, geometry-normalized RGB pupil/iris area fraction. Reference: independently synchronized >=200Hz pupil/iris area plus visible iris fraction.

Frontier: ocular blink/pupil extraction and missingness artifacts are established. Metric: sharp identified-set width, sign certification, false certifications and abstention.

Confound: eyelid visibility correlated with pupil dynamics, plus optical/photometric errors. Model: z=integral(vq)/integral(v), m<=v<=M.

Falsification: independent human reference mean outside a certified interval or a certified increase without reference increase.

Prediction: freeze m/M and photometric error on calibration participants before participant/device/light/glasses holdout. The current frozen prediction is synthetic only.

Human plan: paired RGB and high-speed reference, independent participants/devices, gaze, lighting and glasses variation. Controls: geometry, brightness, missingness, smoothness.

Publication threshold: prospective human boundary crossing, primary prior art, independent checker and reproducibility. Product: calibrated interval/sign or abstain; no fatigue diagnosis.
