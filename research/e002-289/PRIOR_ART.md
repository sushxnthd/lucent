# Primary prior-art and priority rejection
Wang, Chern & Alexa, 2019, Center of circle after perspective transformation: https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.04541 — DIRECT PRIOR ART for two concentric conics and projective pupil/iris radius ratio. This kernel does NOT rediscover it.
Hayes & Petrov, 2016: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4637269/ — gaze-dependent pupil foreshortening and correction.
Entrance pupil optical model, 2019: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-45827-3 — viewing-angle and corneal optical distortion.
CondSeg, 2024: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.17231 — eyelid visibility and ellipse fitting.
RGBE-Gaze, IEEE TPAMI 2025: https://github.com/GuangrongZhao/RGBE-Gaze — synchronized RGB, event stream and gaze references across 66 participants; not accessed here.
mEBAL2: https://github.com/BiDAlab/mEBAL2 — RGB/NIR/manual blink annotations, but no high-speed pupil diameter ground truth.
Priority outcome: ideal projective ratio novelty REJECTED; pursue only calibrated boundary with human falsification.
