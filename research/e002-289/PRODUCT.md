# E002-289 PRODUCT
REJECT: uncalibrated rapid pupil waveform and conic-ratio-as-physiological-proof.
CALIBRATE: independently measure per-device contour uncertainty, registration shift, optical pupil-specific distortion, shutter and timebase. Certify only with positive separation margin; otherwise abstain.
ALLOW FOR RESEARCH: longer, stable, well-lit, visibly segmented pupil/iris ratio sequences.
REDESIGN: if calibrated gates fail, use event-level blink observation with shutter-aware abstention. E002-286 rejects five-second blink-rate-only fatigue classification.
PROHIBIT: claiming known 2019 projective conic invariant as new discovery, or using hypothetical thresholds as product defaults.
