# Prospective test protocol

Primary signal: ordinary-RGB full-image weighted eyelid-edge occupation per exposure. Reference: independent synchronized high-speed anatomical eyelid aperture and edge trace integrated over exact RGB exposure times. Primary targets: normalized mean edge position and threshold occupancy.

Model: calibrated static PSF and spatial response, temporal gain bounded by m and M, ratio r=m/M. Metric: reference coverage of sharp expectile intervals, interval width, and false-positive temporal-sign certification. Strongest confounds: time-varying gaze and temporal illumination, glasses reflections, missing support.

Prespecified controls: geometry-only, brightness-only, missingness-only, temporal smoothness. Hold out participants and devices. Before final reference inspection freeze one mechanism-derived crossing prediction using training-only calibration. Falsify by reference outside certified interval or false certified sign. If any calibration gate fails, abstain.

Human validation has not been run. Do not claim publication novelty or fatigue diagnosis. After two blocked attempts on a data path, switch to theory, synthetic instrument modeling or external validation.
