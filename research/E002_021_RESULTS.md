# E002-021 synthetic findings

Three-replicate, five-second synthetic waveform tests (300 trials per acquisition regime) compared a legacy interpolation-based O4>1.25 ratio to non-interpolating bounded identification intervals. Null O4 passes were 26/300 with full observations, 32/300 at 5% shared dropout, 30/300 at 10%, 38/300 at 20%, and 33/300 with 10% independent dropout. Under assumed valid global smoothness L=0.25/s and absolute noise <=0.001, the interval method certified no null differences and identified the injected 0.02-amplitude positive control in all tested trials. When actual noise exceeded the assumed bound, false certifications reappeared. All results are synthetic, not human RGB or fatigue validation. Original PupilSense scale negative result is preserved. Full code and tests are in the accompanying downloadable E002-021 package.
