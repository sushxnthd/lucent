# Primary-source novelty / priority audit (2026-10-20)

1. Xi Wang, Albert Chern, Marc Alexa (2019), Center of circle after perspective transformation. https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.04541 . **Explicitly anticipates the conic-pencil pupil/iris radius ratio**. Do not claim novelty for the positive invariant.
2. Petersch & Dierkes (2022), Gaze-angle dependency of pupil-size measurements in head-mounted eye tracking, Behavior Research Methods. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9046372/ . Real and synthetic pupil foreshortening/refraction evidence; m is only a simplified nuisance.
3. Geoffrey K. Aguirre (2019), A model of the entrance pupil of the human eye, Scientific Reports. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-45827-3 . Entrance-pupil refraction and viewing-angle effects.
4. Snip & Stitch (2026), a simple and accessible correction for the pupil foreshortening error, Behavior Research Methods. https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-026-02980-8 . Contemporary practical correction comparator.
5. Zhuang Jia et al. (2024), CondSeg: Ellipse Estimation of Pupil and Iris via Conditioned Segmentation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.17231 . Occlusion-aware segmentation comparator, not anatomical ground truth.

**Priority verdict:** The positive invariant is known; the sharp drift gate is elementary interval algebra; full-RGB counterexample is a conditional instrument veto, not proven field-level novelty. A prospective independently calibrated human test with unseen crossing is required.
