# Prior art and novelty boundary

Greene (1986) investigated Poisson blink counts. Bentivoglio and colleagues (1997) established strong task effects on blink rate. Doughty (2016) documented short-term variability. Schleicher and colleagues (2008) studied duration and reopening dynamics as sleepiness markers. Integrate-and-fire models can produce non-Poisson interblink distributions.

The mathematical ingredients here, thinning, superposition, likelihood-ratio testing and total variation, are established. The present contribution is an explicit Lucent measurement rejection criterion, not a new general theorem. Independent human calibration and holdouts remain necessary.
