# E002-176 frozen protocol — 2026-10-14 before human outcomes

## Active primary kernel
Signal: ordinary-RGB normalized pupil/iris temporal change. Reference: independent native-timestamp eye-tracker pupil diameter (mm). Frontier: MEYE (2021) and js-mEye (2025) already compared webcam pupillometry to EyeLink; no untouched Lucent cross-device waveform success. Scalar observability: participant-bootstrap 95% lower Pearson r > 0.30, separate lag, amplitude, NRMSE and coverage gates. Strongest confound: illumination-dependent pupil boundary bias, gaze foreshortening, eyelid occlusion. Decisive falsification: held-out RGB fails frozen gates or strongest brightness/geometry nuisance controls explain it. Publishable success: >=8 unseen participants, >0.10 correlation advantage over nuisance, independent checker, prior-art audit and all waveform gates. Product threshold: calibrated uncertainty/rejection with useful accepted coverage. Kill: 3 nondiscriminating runs or prospective failure; do not claim five-second fatigue.

## Auxiliary necessary-condition experiment (NOT an RGB experiment)
Question: does ideal 30-fps sampling alone destroy the low-frequency (0–5 Hz) structure in original 120-Hz human pupil-diameter references?
Source: MCFW-Gaze v3 (2026-05-20), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20300972, dataset.zip published MD5 5e04a7b5e50f7508775e1daea527246b. Require MD5 and record SHA256. Exactly one image_01_1.tsv per participant, 15 participant IDs; do not silently substitute.
Use native 120-Hz timestamp and explicit Tobii SDK pupil-validity (true/1 valid). If schema or validity ambiguous, fail, never assume. Binocular mean diameter in mm; disjoint 5-s windows; reject >5% invalid or gaps >3 nominal frames. Detrend and zero-phase 5-Hz Butterworth lowpass on reference (retrospective idealized upper-bound only). Ideal point-sample at 30 fps for 4 phases, interpolate to 120 Hz, exclude 0.2s boundaries. Record Pearson correlations per phase, worst-phase r, 0–5Hz spectral power fraction, window acceptance, failures.
Auxiliary scalar: fraction of eligible 5-s windows with worst-phase r>=0.90. Sampling-only success: >=8 participants, >=70% eligible windows, >=90% passing, participant bootstrap 95% lower fraction >=80%. Not a physiological success or deployability claim.
Product consequence if passes: 30fps nominal rate alone is unlikely to be principal bottleneck for 0–5Hz; prioritize actual optics/photometry and paired capture. If fails: study rate/exposure and reject insufficient cameras, not claim RGB impossibility. Wildcard: anomalous binocular disagreement only, no frozen-gate tuning.
