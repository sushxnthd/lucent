# Lucent product gate — 2026-10-21

Allowed as research candidates: native-rate RGB blink-occurrence detection with acquisition timestamps and explicit abstention; calibrated geometry-normalized eyelid-aperture trends. All statements require device- and participant-stratified validation before release.

Rejected: five-second fatigue diagnosis or certification; recovery of unrestricted sub-exposure pupil waveforms; precise blink peak velocity at ordinary 30 fps; blink completeness from 10-Hz decimation; physiological-lag point estimates without clock calibration.

Required redesign: preserve native frames, exposure timing, gain/white-balance metadata where available, dropped-frame flags and uncertainty. Treat unobservable quantities as intervals or abstain. EOG reference can validate blink occurrence, **not full closure duration** without independent eyelid measurement.

No product claim is upgraded by this protocol-only run.
