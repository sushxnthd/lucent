# Active question and frozen gates

Signal: ordinary RGB pupil-to-anatomical-iris temporal ratio. Reference: synchronized same-eye tracker pupil diameter in millimeters. Scalar observability: lower 95% participant-bootstrap Pearson correlation bound, with Pearson r >0.30 and >0.10 above nuisance, lag <=100ms, amplitude ratio 0.5-1.5, NRMSE <=0.75. Strongest confounds: brightness-induced boundary drift, gaze projection, blur, camera/reference clock drift. Falsification: untouched participant/device/lighting/glasses holdouts fail frozen waveform or nuisance gates. Publishable: >=8 untouched paired participants, independent checker, prior-art audit and mechanism-derived unseen prediction. Product: >=75% prospective acceptance with calibrated rejection. Kill or reformulate after prospective waveform failure or three nondiscriminating primary runs. This run only audits source eligibility; it does not revise the primary gates or make a fatigue claim.
