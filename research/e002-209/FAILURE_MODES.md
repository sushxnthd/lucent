# Original-human five-second reference failure mechanisms

Independent replay of E002-208 derived artifact, 15 participants per predeclared metadata-only stratum. Same-eye eligibility requires >=570 positive-valid pupil samples, >=95% validity, and <=26 ms maximum gap including both boundaries.

Low: 0/15 sample-count eligible, 0/15 old-gap passing, 0/15 full-gap passing.
Median: 8/15 sample-count eligible, 2/15 old-gap passing, 1/15 full-gap passing. Six count-eligible participants fail interior continuity; one further fails boundary continuity.
High: 15/15 count eligible, 15/15 old-gap passing, 14/15 full-gap passing; one fails boundary continuity.

This shows whole-trial validity fraction is not a sufficient five-second continuity certificate. The high-stratum 14/15 is selected with tracker metadata and cannot be interpreted as prospective RGB product coverage. No RGB videos or physiological waveform results were evaluated.
