# Primary prior-art audit (2026-10-18)

- Soukupova and Cech, *Real-Time Eye Blink Detection using Facial Landmarks* (2016): existing eye-aspect-ratio and landmark blink detection. https://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/ftp/articles/cech/Soukupova-TR-2016-05.pdf
- Wolter, Berrio Perez and Shan, *Blinking Beyond EAR: A Stable Eyelid Angle Metric for Driver Drowsiness Detection and Data Augmentation* (2025 preprint): 3D landmark eyelid angle designed for camera-angle robustness. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19519
- Culemann et al., *Blink parameters are confounded by vertical eye orientation in video-based eye tracking* (20 March 2026), Behavior Research Methods 58:89, DOI 10.3758/s13428-026-02984-4. Human evidence about **eye-in-head gaze**, distinct from head pitch. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-026-02984-4
- Wen et al., *Accurate Real-time 3D Gaze Tracking Using a Lightweight Eyeball Calibration* (2020): monocular eye geometry calibration is established. https://diglib.eg.org/items/05bfea1c-50ba-4102-b01c-d02a5e617f4d

**Collision verdict:** This exact conditional set-membership derivation does not establish a new field-level mathematical theorem or an independently validated ocular sensing capability.
