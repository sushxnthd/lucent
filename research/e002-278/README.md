# E002-278: exact finite-sample RGB nuisance-separation boundary

2026-10-18. **Evidence: exact conditional theorem + deterministic synthetic instrument. No human RGB result.**

## One active measurement kernel
**Question:** Can a known stimulus-locked log(pupil/iris radius) template be distinguished from all sufficiently slow measurement artifacts?

1. Signal/reference: RGB apparent log pupil/iris radius vs synchronized same-eye eye-tracker log diameter change (reference not used here).
2. Literature: gaze and refraction artifacts are known (Hayes & Petrov 2016; Petersch & Dierkes 2022); pupil timing is sample-rate dependent (Bergamin & Kardon 2003). Lipschitz set-membership identification is standard mathematics.
3. Metric: exact worst-case amplitude threshold Acrit and margin |A|/Acrit-1.
4. Confound: pupil-specific optical/segmentation drift that mimics physiology.
5. Model: raw frames result from exposure-integrated geometry, eyelid aperture, gaze, PSF, lighting and optics; positive extracted q=apparent pupil radius/iris radius. Under a *restricted multiplicative* nuisance regime z=log(q)=c+A*sbar+b+e, with |b(t)-b(u)|<=L|t-u|, |e_k|<=eps. The log-template sbar includes exposure integration. L must bound ALL optical/brightness/gaze/aperture residuals. Arbitrary additive contour bias invalidates the model.
6. Falsification: explicit identical-observation response/no-response witness whenever |A|<=Acrit.
7. Boundary: Acrit=min_{i<j,ds!=0}(2L*dt+4eps)/|ds|; infinity when all sampled template values coincide. Includes sampling alias and exposure attenuation.
8. Human test: pending. Freeze L,eps,clock,template on calibration participants; test untouched participants/devices/light/gaze/glasses. Controls: geometry-only, brightness-only, missingness-only, smoothness-only; reference-blind admission.
9. Publication threshold: independent proof and checker, primary prior-art collision audit, calibrated RGB nuisance bounds, prospective human holdouts, nontrivial new empirical/product consequence.
10. Product: only conditional known-probe response detection and abstention. No unconstrained anatomical waveform or fatigue inference.

## Exact conditional theorem and constructive proof
H0:A=0 and H1:A=a produce intersecting possible observations iff **|a(s_j-s_i)| <= 2L|t_j-t_i|+4eps for every i,j**. The difference g of two admissible nuisance sequences is 2L-Lipschitz and within 2eps of a*s. Conversely g_i=max_j(a*s_j-2eps-2L|t_i-t_j|) is 2L-Lipschitz and within 2eps of a*s_i. Choosing b0=g/2,b1=-g/2,e0=(a*s-g)/2,e1=-e0 gives exactly the same observed z=a*s/2. The inequality is necessary and sufficient; pairwise, not merely adjacent, tests are needed with eps>0. This is **binary separation for a KNOWN template**, not arbitrary waveform reconstruction.

## SCIENCE ledger
Illustrative, NOT calibrated: L=.012 log units/s, eps=.0015 log units, five seconds, A=.0048 log units (~0.48%). A 24fps 4Hz sinusoid with 1/48s exposure has Acrit=.0046719884, hence disjoint response/no-response observation classes. At 1/24s exposure Acrit=.0048367983, hence an identical-observation counterexample exists. At 10fps, 5Hz, phase zero, sampled sine is identically zero: Acrit=infinity. The exposure boundary was selected after exploratory parameter scanning, NOT a prospectively untouched empirical prediction. Full raw witness generated by experiment.py; no human physiology demonstrated.
