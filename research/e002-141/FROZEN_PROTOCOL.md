H1: Oracle mask pupil/visible-eye area ratio is more robust to fractional BOX than NN at 32x16 and 16x8 (prior-art expectation). H2: Fixed RGB luminance threshold using oracle eye mask fails to generalize as well as oracle geometry. H3: sequence-specific failure reversals persist.

For RGB threshold baseline, use oracle eye region as an optimistic (nondeployable) constraint. Normalize RGB luminance by 5th and 95th eye-region percentiles; select threshold from 0..1 step .05 on development frames to maximize mean pupil Dice at 64x32 (ties favor lower). Freeze and evaluate holdouts at all sizes. Raw luminance threshold same calibration and reporting. No tuning on holdouts.
Metrics: per-sequence median/p90 abs(relative ratio error), threshold Dice, validity, zero-pupil rate, clipping; aggregate sequence medians (not frame pooling).
