# Primary prior-art anchors (partial, 2026-10-20)

Raskar, Agrawal, Tumblin, *Coded Exposure Photography: Motion Deblurring Using Fluttered Shutter*, ACM TOG (2006), https://www.merl.com/publications/TR2006-124 — temporal exposure weighting already known.

Meingast, Geyer, Sastry, *Geometric Models of Rolling-Shutter Cameras* (2005), https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0503076 — row-time geometry excluded here.

Ghosal and Mehrotra, *Orthogonal moment operators for subpixel edge detection* (1993), DOI 10.1016/0031-3203(93)90038-X — edge localization prior art.

*Subpixel Localization of Isolated Edges and Streaks in Digital Images* (2020), https://www.mdpi.com/2313-433X/6/5/33 — blur/noise localization prior art.

*Analysis of Spontaneous Eyelid Blink Dynamics Using a 240 Frames per Second Smartphone Camera* (2019), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30893187/ — human high-speed blink data.

Novelty verdict: small exact specialization of known weighted-median and coded-exposure principles; no publication-level novelty without prospective human boundary validation.
