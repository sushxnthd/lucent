## Pre-existing interface incompatibility (not a finding of human behavior)
The E002-598 offline reviewer exports `measurement_x` for measured frames and deliberately exports `iris_diameter:null`. E002-576's evaluator requires the exact legacy six-key schema including a numeric iris diameter, and it requires 330 held-out clips, whereas E002-598 native packets contain ten development clips. Directly feeding E002-598 exports into E002-576 must fail. A bridge must not fabricate iris diameters or silently substitute development frames for participant-disjoint holdout.

## Geometry hypothesis and kill test
E002-598 previously records the y coordinate of upper and lower clicks without checking whether those clicks were made at the chosen `measurement_x`. A slanted pair of eyelid margins can therefore produce an apparent closure from two points at different columns even though the eye is open at every column. Predeclared counterexample: upper margin u(x)=100+0.5x, lower margin l(x)=130+0.5x, so true aperture l(x)-u(x)=30 px everywhere. At x=58, true aperture=30; upper clicked at x=58 gives y=129; lower clicked at x=0 gives y=130; the old interface stores apparent aperture=1 px at measurement_x=58. For independently calibrated iris diameter 100 px, true normalized aperture=0.30 (>0.20 OPEN) but apparent normalized aperture=0.01 (<=0.20 CLOSED). This is a synthetic geometry falsification, not a human observation.

## Predeclared acceptance gates
1. Modify the E002-598 click handler to refuse an upper/lower click whose x differs by more than 1.0 native image pixel from the selected measurement column. A refused click must not change saved coordinates; the user must select a new column or retry. The 1.0-px threshold is a UI registration requirement, **not** a physiological/geometry error bound.
2. Add a separate E002-599 native-only two-rater adjudicator for exactly ten blind clips x 19 frames, consuming actual E002-598 native export shapes. Require two distinct rater labels, complete frame mapping, numeric finite y/e/x for unflagged frames, iris_diameter=null in all E002-598 rows, and explicit nuisance abstention. Never fill missing coordinates or infer source identity.
3. Require a **separate owner-supplied calibration** per blind clip: iris diameter d, conservative diameter error delta with 0<delta<d, registered reference column x_ref and maximum allowed registration deviation <=1 px. The independent provenance flags and external reference ID are assertions only, never automatically authenticated. Without any calibration or if either rater's x differs from x_ref by more than allowed deviation, classify UNCERTAIN. If the two raters' columns differ by more than 1 px, classify DISAGREEMENT or UNCERTAIN, never CLOSED/OPEN.
4. Construct per-rater normalized aperture intervals [max(0,l-u-2e)/(d+delta), max(0,l-u+2e)/(d-delta)] and require overlap and both entire intervals strictly above threshold 0.20 for OPEN or both at/below 0.20 for CLOSED. Other cases abstain. Do not treat image indices as instantaneous continuous-time truth.
5. Only report positive *native-frame index* witnesses when two retained OPEN endpoints enclose a CLOSED unretained frame in each of three predeclared E002-595 phase maps: [0,3,6,10,13,16], [1,4,7,11,14,17], [2,5,8,12,15,18]. Do not infer a negative closure certificate from zero witnesses. The three phases are not complete continuous-phase coverage (E002-596).
6. Synthetic regression must cover a positive witness, cross-column apparent-closure attack, missing calibration, threshold uncertainty, rater disagreement, invalid/missing frame, nuisance abstention, and non-identifying aggregate output. The test must fail under an intentionally permissive x check and pass with the registered-column gate. No real ratings/images in code, CI artifacts, or repository.
7. If any gate fails, keep the interface and the evaluator research-only, preserve negative results, and do not promote product claims. Even if all gates pass, physical shutter, clock, hard motion envelope, blinded human labels and held-out physical predictions remain absent.
