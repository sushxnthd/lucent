# E002-516 | Primary literature does not certify a hard human eyelid-velocity cap

Date: 2026-10-29. Frozen eligibility criteria: [E002_516_FROZEN.md](E002_516_FROZEN.md), committed first at `c876aa8`. This is a **primary-literature priority audit and product-claim falsification**, not a new physiological theorem, measured physical experiment, held-out human validation, or a public-data benchmark.

## Decisive finding

**No reviewed source establishes the continuous-time, population-wide, geometry-matched upper speed bound needed to turn the sharp E002-371 conditional shutter-gap law into a deterministic human hidden-closure exclusion.** In particular, the maximum speed *observed* in a finite high-speed recording is not a certified maximum of the unobserved trajectory between samples or of a future participant. This is a limitation of the evidence and inference, not a claim that human eyelid motion has unbounded physical speed.

**Evidence caution:** The 243 mm/s figure is a study's *mean peak*, not a maximum across all human blinks. The 160 mm/s upper value is the reported range in one small spontaneous cohort, not a physiological ceiling. Doane's occasional >400 mm/s observation does not establish 400 mm/s as a ceiling either. All three quantities are different estimands; comparing them as if they were the same bound is invalid.

## Why finite sampled speed observations cannot discharge the theorem's assumption

Let `a(t)` be the exact dimensionless scalar aperture used by the theorem and `V` the stipulated uniform bound `|da/dt| <= V` in s^-1. Published physical velocities `v_mm` (mm/s) only map to `V = v_mm / D_mm` when `D_mm > 0` is an **independently measured** matching physical scale and the physical displacement exactly defines the scalar aperture. Uncertainty in `D_mm`, perspective, eyelid edge, gaze and rolling-shutter row mapping must be propagated. Angular velocities cannot be substituted without a calibrated geometric model.

Even noiseless samples at 600 fps do not by themselves upper-bound the derivative between samples. Two smooth, bounded aperture functions can agree at every sampled timestamp while one contains a narrow within-gap excursion. Making that excursion narrower increases its peak derivative without changing any sampled values. This is an **elementary finite-sampling identifiability observation**, not a new theorem and not proof that such arbitrarily fast trajectories are physiologically possible. An independent physical regularity assumption is precisely what the E002-371 theorem needs.

A bound derived from the *same* held-out video under evaluation is circular. The independent speed bound, if ever justified, must be frozen from external calibration/physiology before opening the held-out closure labels.

## Immediate scientific and product consequences

1. **Reject** product copy such as “Lucent proves you did not blink between frames,” “600-fps literature certifies human maximum eyelid speed,” or “30-fps video is sufficient for all people.” The literature supports none of those claims.
2. Preserve E002-371 as a **sharp conditional mathematical law** only: with externally justified geometry, aperture, exposure/clock, and continuous-time speed assumptions, a given shutter gap may exclude a hidden closure. If any assumption is unresolved, return `ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED`.
3. Distinguish two future claims: (a) **conditional physical certificate** restricted to a demonstrably bounded device/subject/condition; (b) **statistical false-exclusion risk** for participant-disjoint, prospectively defined certificates with abstention coverage reported. E002-511's certificate-conditional sample-size constraint remains applicable; a small pilot cannot imply 1% population risk.
4. Freeze nuisance strata **before** physical testing: spontaneous/voluntary/reflex, gaze angle, participant, glasses/occlusion, eyelid/iris geometry, illumination, blur, dropped frames, rolling shutter, and independent clock. A speed cap calibrated only in one stratum does not transfer by default.
5. The **next non-synthetic evidence** remains: two genuine independent blinded S01 anatomical reviewers; independently calibrated exposure start/end and line delay from physical instrumentation; and participant-disjoint high-speed reference with prespecified hidden-closure predictions. E002-515 is an input evaluator, not an attestation mechanism.

## Priority boundary and completion status

The publishable question cannot be “first high-speed blink measurement” or “first LED rolling-shutter calibration.” Those are established. The narrower potentially original result is a **sharp, falsifiable observability boundary for hidden events under explicit acquisition and physiology constraints, plus calibrated abstention**. Priority of the precise E002-371 mathematical result is **not established by this targeted audit**; a broader theorem/identifiability literature search is still required before claiming novelty.

No physical recordings, anatomical ratings, certified speed bounds or held-out physical predictions were acquired. This run changes the permitted claim interpretation and research design, not the evidence tier. **Product `ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED`; release `BLOCKED`.** Hush unchanged.
