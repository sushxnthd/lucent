# E002-554 targeted primary-literature priority check (2026-10-31)

This is a **bounded** literature check for the shutter-calibration identifiability falsifier, not a claim of exhaustive novelty or another human-data search.

1. **Oth, Furgale, Kneip & Siegwart (CVPR 2013), "Rolling Shutter Camera Calibration"**. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2013/html/Oth_Rolling_Shutter_Camera_2013_CVPR_paper.html . Existing calibration of shutter timing from a known pattern, with real-camera experiments. Therefore neither LED/target-based timing calibration nor the need to model rolling-shutter timing is novel to Lucent.
2. **Dong et al. (Applied Optics 2022), "Parametric modeling and experimental measurement of rolling shutter characteristics for optical camera communication using undersampled modulation."** https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.468009 . Prior parametric temporal transfer-function measurement; supports treating temporal response as an experimentally measurable transfer function, not assuming a universal boxcar.
3. **Huai et al. (Optics Letters 2023), "Automated rolling shutter calibration with an LED panel."** https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.474375 . Demonstrates practical LED-based interline-delay calibration on industrial/consumer cameras. It estimates line delay, not (from its abstract alone) a distribution-free shutter second-moment certificate.
4. **Kurosawa et al. (Japanese Association of Forensic Science and Technology 2024), "Development of a device to measure the temporal imaging characteristics of CMOS/CCD cameras."** https://doi.org/10.3408/jafst.875 . Demonstrates LED instrumentation for frame interval, exposure time and scanning speed; again these measured quantities must not be conflated with certified full temporal sensitivity shape.

**Positioning:** E002-554 is an exact classical inverse-problem counterexample showing that *a specific incomplete finite-offset pulse protocol* does not identify the second moment required by E002-553. Do not claim novelty for shutter timing calibration, rolling shutter, LED metrology, nonidentifiability of undersampled functions or Jensen/curvature inequalities. The possible research contribution is an independently physically validated, nuisance-robust **decision boundary** connecting measured shutter-kernel uncertainty to human eyelid closure observability. That evidence does not yet exist.

**Priority-audit limitation:** Abstracts/accessible publication metadata were checked, not a systematic full-text prior-art review. This does not establish first discovery or publication priority.
