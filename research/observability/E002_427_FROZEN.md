# E002-427 — prospective 400-Hz human-kinematics transfer protocol (frozen 2026-10-25)

**Primary kernel:** E002-371 shutter-gap/eyelid-velocity observability. **Evidence tier at freeze:** public primary-literature human kinematics, plus a planned **synthetic virtual-camera** adapter. No restricted individual-level human traces, independently calibrated shutter, or new human annotations were available. This is a human-validation instrument, not a physical validation result.

## New physical evidence and access decision

1. **FaceBase Clites/Kim/Rootman, released 2026-09-01:** eight adults, five eyelid behaviors, synchronized 3D upper/lower eyelid-margin marker motion at **400 frames/s** and intramuscular EMG. Dataset DOI **10.25550/88-28BJ**, associated PNAS 2025 paper DOI **10.1073/pnas.2508058122**. Individual-level data are **controlled access**, limited to General Research Use / Not-for-profit Use Only. Do **not** attempt to bypass FaceBase's Data Access Request and Data Use Certification requirements; data must not enter public GitHub or public CI. Source: https://trackhub.facebase.org/2026/09/01/recent-release-clites-eyelid-kinematics-emg/ ; access: https://docs.facebase.org/docs/human-data/
2. **Kwon et al., J. R. Soc. Interface 2013, DOI 10.1098/rsif.2013.0227:** 25 volunteers filmed at **600 frames/s**; central palpebral aperture was measured every **5 ms**, not every camera frame. Mean reported peak closing speed **243 ± 9 mm/s** and mean initial aperture **9.83 ± 0.17 mm**; the paper compares prior reported individual-group mean speeds up to **427 ± 30 mm/s**. These are descriptive population/sample measurements, **not physiological hard velocity bounds**. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4043155/
3. The earlier Eye-BCI S01/S19 clips have human-eye imagery but no independently validated physical aperture. The two E002-417 pixel-gap estimators were rejected. Do not recycle them as ground truth.

**Access verdict:** FaceBase is the strongest newly identified high-rate marker-based human resource for a kinematic transfer test, but individual traces are not currently accessible under the required approval. The 2013 paper provides measured human speed scale, not independent trial-level ground truth for a held-out experiment.

## Preregistered adapter and falsification

- Input only a **deidentified, privately stored** normalized CSV with participant pseudonym, strictly increasing physical timestamps, independently registered **central anatomical aperture in millimeters**, valid flag, and eight explicit nuisance flags: brightness, blur, gaze, glasses, occlusion, missingness, clock, geometry. **Do not use raw 3D marker distance as aperture**: anatomical correspondence and optical/3D registration require separate validation.
- Config must declare nonempty, disjoint training/holdout participant sets, source and physical-registration references, closure threshold in mm, hypothetical camera and reference velocity caps (mm/s), max 400-Hz sample spacing, physical-aperture uncertainty, camera clock error, virtual frame period and exposure width. A reference string is not proof of physical provenance.
- Use **only holdout subjects** to generate **idealized virtual boxcar camera measurements** by exact integration of the piecewise-linear 400-Hz aperture interpolant. If any covered sample is missing, flagged, or farther apart than the maximum allowed reference step, abstain. No real RGB shutter transfer is implied.
- Apply the restricted E002-422 balanced-slope bound only when its inequality remains valid after measurement, interpolation, and clock-error allowances. Compute the conservative lower aperture; classify a *study-only conditional candidate* when it strictly exceeds the threshold, otherwise abstain.
- For the independent high-rate reference, count an unambiguous, nuisance-free sampled closure as **WITNESSED_CROSSING**, even if other frames are missing. Absence of sampled closure is **UNRESOLVED** unless a separately justified reference velocity cap plus annotation and endpoint-error envelopes excludes all between-sample crossings. Never equate 400-Hz sampling with continuous-time truth.
- Report per-participant observed secant speeds, violations of the hypothesized camera speed cap, candidate count, witnessed failures, unresolved references, abstentions, and sharp **finite-evaluation-set** error identification interval. Overlapping virtual intervals are dependent, not independent samples. The adapter is exploratory; the existing E002-426 prospective seal must precede any real held-out reference examination.
- **Adversarial gates:** a synthetic 400-mm/s narrow closure must falsify a wrongly assumed 180-mm/s camera bound at 30 Hz; any nuisance/missing data must trigger abstention; a 400-Hz trace without an independently justified reference cap must not be interpreted as a definitive negative; invalid participant split or numeric input must be rejected.
- **Product gate:** every output remains `ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED`, with `physical_certificate_authorized=false`, regardless of apparent synthetic success.

## Human execution and go/no-go

1. Obtain FaceBase controlled-data approval (or independent consented 400–600-Hz marker/video data). Preserve original timestamps, calibration, and restrictions. Verify whether marker positions can be registered to a **central physical aperture** with bounded error; if not, classify the dataset as *kinematic marker motion only* and reject the physical-aperture transfer claim.
2. Separate training from holdout by **participant**, and if available by capture device. Obtain two genuinely independent blinded raters for anatomical registration and closure adjudication. Estimate measurement uncertainty from repeatability, optical geometry, gaze, and occlusion; document rolling-shutter/clock calibration independently.
3. Freeze speed-hypothesis sensitivity grid and virtual 24/30/60/120-Hz exposure design before holdout analysis. A finite sample maximum is never a hard physiological cap; all conditional negatives must identify that assumption.
4. On unseen human sequences, score witnessed contradictions and unresolved reference fractions, report all participant and nuisance strata, and attempt independent replication. Only independently authenticated physical camera/exposure/geometry data can advance beyond **virtual-camera kinematic validation**.
5. If FaceBase access is not granted, run the frozen synthetic falsification and leave physical validation blocked; do not continue dataset archaeology or synthetic theorem polishing.

**Novelty boundary:** the shutter-gap law is an optimal-recovery special case; 400-Hz motion capture and 600-fps human blink measurement are existing literature. The prospective contribution, if real human data become available, is an independently falsifiable *camera-observability* measurement protocol with honest uncertainty and abstention, not a claim of newly discovered human physiology.
