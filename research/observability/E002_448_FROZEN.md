# E002-448 — frozen blinded human anatomical-annotation bridge (2026-10-26)

**Frozen before E002-448 implementation or new human-frame inspection.** Parent E002-371; S19 is pilot only. S21/S22 remain sealed. This is a measurement-annotation instrument, **not** a physiological velocity bound or physical shutter certification.

## Hypothesis and sampling
The previously hash-verified S19 Phantom AVI and candidate sync event list may be used to construct an offline blinded anatomical annotation pack. Work only in the **first 12,000 decoded frames**. Candidate event gaps are an *unverified index-alignment hypothesis*, not physical exposure intervals. Select **all 10–100 ms candidate gaps**, capped at 24 in chronological order, and an equal number of <=10 ms controls selected by SHA256(seed || index), from frame indices [2,11998]. If there are no medium gaps, fail rather than invent positives. For each selected pair (i-1,i), export indices (i-2,i-1,i) as individual 320x288 grayscale PNGs. Deduplicate indices, then add 12 hidden repeat views of deterministic hashed-selected frames. Use the fixed published seed only for control-frame selection. For actual blinding, generate a fresh cryptographically random per-pack private key and use keyed HMAC-SHA256 for opaque view IDs and view order. Preserve the private key in the offline master manifest only; do not disclose it to raters. Reproducibility is via the retained private master, not reversible public hashes. Keep the index-to-ID key **only in the local private master manifest**, never in the rater packs or CI artifacts.

## Rater task and measurement
Two independent blinded human raters mark **left and right canthi and upper/lower eyelid margin points** in image pixels on each individual frame, with no timing, gap class, or adjacent-frame ordering revealed. Explicit reject options: not visibly anatomical, occlusion/glasses, glare/brightness, motion blur, off-axis gaze, ambiguous upper/lower boundary, and other. No model-generated landmarks are ground truth. Require all four landmarks, canthal baseline >=20 pixels, upper and lower points' canthal-axis projections within 0.15 baseline units, and plausible normalized perpendicular aperture in [0,1.5]. Derived normalized aperture is the perpendicular upper-to-lower displacement divided by canthal length, with the positive direction fixed by upper/lower labels. It is a **dimensionless 2-D image measurement**, not physical mm, not a perspective-corrected anatomical distance.

## Quality and adjudication
A valid frame requires two independent completed rater records, no nuisance flags, repeat-view self-consistency <=0.03 normalized aperture, and between-rater aperture discrepancy <=0.03. Failure of any gate yields **ABSTAIN_REFERENCE_UNRESOLVED**. Never treat abstention as a witnessed open frame. Predeclared reporting: number of candidate gaps/controls, unique frames/views, complete annotations, eligible consensus frames, repeated-view disagreements, between-rater disagreements, eligible pairs, and distribution of dimensionless aperture. For no completed human raters, report **ZERO_HUMAN_LABELS**, not measured aperture.

## Security and evidence tiers
Never commit, log, or upload human images, per-frame index mapping, individual annotations, or private master manifests. Rater packs must be generated and used offline, under dataset license and applicable human-subjects/privacy approvals. A CI job may download and checksum-verify S19, generate then discard local images, and publish only a nonidentifying count summary. Synthetic fixtures test coordinate geometry, blind IDs, nuisance abstention, repeat inconsistency, invalid/missing annotations, and strict no-physical-release behavior. S21/S22 must not be read, even for debugging.

## Decision
This instrument enables genuine human anatomical labels if independently supplied; it does not itself produce them. Even complete rater agreement cannot authenticate shutter timing, physical scale, a hard human velocity cap, or E002-371. Product hidden-closure, blink duration, PERCLOS, fatigue and medical claims remain `ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED`. Preserve negative outcomes and draft PR; do not merge.
