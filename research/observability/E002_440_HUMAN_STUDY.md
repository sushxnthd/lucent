# E002-440 — prospective participant-cluster human study and release handoff

**Status:** study protocol only. No participants acquired. Institutional ethics/consent and authorized data access are prerequisites. No raw identifiable human recordings belong in public GitHub or hosted CI.

## Primary estimand and frozen challenge
For each independently recruited participant, challenge the preregistered Lucent model with **exactly 40 predicted no-hidden-closure candidate intervals**, chosen before viewing high-speed reference labels and using a fixed distribution of camera conditions. A participant fails if **any** candidate contains an independently witnessed physical closure. A participant passes only if all 40 candidate intervals have complete, blinded, independently authenticated reference-negative adjudication. Any missing, ambiguous or unauthenticated reference interval makes the participant unresolved and blocks a population release claim. If the model cannot produce 40 candidates for a participant, report the shortfall/abstention and do not count that participant as a zero-error success. This is an intentionally demanding fixed-candidate estimand; it does not characterize overall model coverage.

## Required physical evidence
Follow [E002-433](E002_433_HUMAN_HANDOFF.md), [E002-436](E002_436_RESULT.md) and [E002-439](E002_439_HUMAN_HANDOFF.md) for synchronized raw exposure and optical/photodiode timing, calibrated 3D aperture, two blinded anatomical raters, and uncertainty propagation. Never substitute a published mean peak blink velocity for a hard individual bound. Include spontaneous, voluntary and reflex events and crossed head pose, gaze, brightness, blur, glasses, shutter, frame loss, clock error and occlusion; stratify these precommitted challenges and report failures and abstention per stratum.

## Statistical analysis
The independent sampling unit is the participant, not frames, eyes or repeated sessions. For n fully evaluable independently sampled participants and k with >=1 witnessed false certificate, report the exact one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson upper confidence bound for the **participant-level any-error probability**, conditional on the tested distribution and independent participants. Do not silently condition on having complete reference labels if reference completeness depends on eyelid behavior. Report unresolved participants and acquisition failures separately; any such exclusions block the release gate absent a separately justified missing-data analysis. For zero errors, U=1-0.05^(1/n). To claim U<1% with zero errors requires at least **299 independent, fully evaluable participants**, not 299 intervals. Nuisance-stratum-specific claims require their own sufficient independent participant support; pooled success does not certify a rare condition. Any witnessed false certificate falsifies a deterministic E002-371 claim under its stated premises, even if population risk is small.

## Precommitment and review
Freeze the model SHA, exact 40-candidate selection rule, threshold, physical speed/acceleration calibration, participant inclusion/exclusion, strata, optical timing calibration, reference adjudication, analysis code and risk target before seeing held-out outcomes. Independent external reviewers must authenticate underlying physical calibration files, not simply tick booleans in the ledger. Use `e002_440_cluster_risk.py --ledger /private/ledger.json` only on an authorized local machine. Its `eligible_for_external_release_review_ONLY` output is a **review queue**, not deployment authorization; `physical_release_authorized` is always false.

## Feasibility and stopping
If independent calibrated physical data or a hard speed bound remain unavailable, publish the negative/abstention evidence and keep physical product claims `ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED`. A smaller pilot (e.g. 20–30 participants) can test annotation and acquisition feasibility but cannot substantiate a <1% participant-level failure risk. Do not relabel 1,200 correlated video intervals as 1,200 independent human trials.
