## Scientific decision and scope

E002-541/542 used synthetic coded-light illumination to identify a missing test: a fitted shutter model may recover the right nominal phase/exposure while failing to predict a held-out optical intervention. E002-543 is the **first owner-private real-capture input path** for that one-sided falsification. It is not a new shutter theorem, physical calibration, physiological validation, or certificate.

Prediction: if a photodiode-timed flash is *strictly outside* the camera model's conservative row-specific shutter support (including independent synchronization uncertainty), and the simple shutter-only optical transfer model is correct, a matched control and flashed frame should not differ beyond a **predeclared independent** bound for noise, illumination drift, and nuisance variation. A larger measured difference is a **candidate falsification of that transfer model**, not proof of incorrect mechanical shutter timing: sensor persistence, flare, rolling-row mixing, ISP and other optical paths may explain it. Failure to see a difference is **censored**, not certification of a valid shutter kernel.

All must be literal booleans. `partition` must be `heldout`. Identifiers must be nonempty and unique; no extra keys or silent defaults. No NaN, Infinity or boolean numeric coercion.

## Frozen decision rules

1. Reject the entire session as `ABSTAIN_INVALID_SESSION` for missing/extra fields, invalid digests, invalid session clock/sample rate, empty trials, duplicate trial IDs or duplicate (frame ID, row ID) pairs. The program cannot authenticate a self-asserted digest, clock, quality flag or physical measurement.
2. Each valid trial requires positive duration for the flash and shutter intervals under interval uncertainty: pulse onset maximum < pulse offset minimum, shutter start maximum < shutter end minimum. ROI values must be finite within [0,1], noise upper must be finite and nonnegative, and all ten quality flags must be true. Otherwise that trial is `ABSTAIN_QUALITY` (not removed from denominator).
3. The uncertainty-expanded pulse is **provably outside** the claimed shutter support only if `pulse_offset_max + sync_error_ms < shutter_start_min` or `pulse_onset_min - sync_error_ms > shutter_end_max`. Equality, overlap or unknown uncertainty yields `ABSTAIN_NOT_OUTSIDE`. Do not infer timing from video FPS, frame IDs or file PTS.
4. For a qualified outside trial, `abs(flash_roi - control_roi) > noise_upper` produces `TRANSFER_FALSIFICATION_CANDIDATE`; equality or smaller difference produces `NO_FALSIFICATION_CENSORED`. No pass/certified-true state exists. The noise upper must be independently predeclared from separate controls; this CLI only checks its syntax, not its calibration.
5. Return aggregate counts for all four trial decisions, the total denominator, and `release: BLOCKED`, `product: ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED`, `evidence_tier: OWNER_SUPPLIED_OPTICAL_RECORDS_UNAUTHENTICATED`. Even if candidates appear, physical verification requires external review of raw synchronized photodiode and native camera evidence.
6. An exact boundary, extra/missing field, malformed timing interval, false quality flag, bad hash, duplicate identity, wrong partition, or self-described but absent optical source must never yield an accepted outside trial.
