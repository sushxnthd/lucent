# E002-537 FROZEN | Participant-level calibration cannot be inferred from window counts

Date: 2026-10-30. Lucent E002-371 primary kernel; existing `research/observability-human-boundary` branch, draft PR #26. **Evidence tier: mathematical finite-sample risk protocol and preregistered synthetic stress, not human anatomy or camera calibration.** This is a decision-rule calibration study, not a new blink detector or claim of a novel conformal theorem. Do not reopen E002-534/535 pixel-return thresholds.

## Scientific question and prediction

The existing E002-483 physical/human pilot specifies 12 calibration participants and 12 participant-disjoint holdouts. Does that design support a nontrivial, distribution-free **95% marginal participant-level** guarantee that a newly sampled participant will experience **no falsely excluded independently verified hidden closure** across all of their eligible windows? The answer is predicted **NO** at n=12: for split conformal with one score per participant and miscoverage alpha=1/20, the required order-statistic index is `k=ceil((n+1)*(1-alpha))=13>12`, hence threshold `+infinity` and universal abstention. The smallest calibration sample permitting a finite order statistic is **n=19**, where k=19 and the threshold is the calibration maximum. Even a finite threshold is only marginal under participant exchangeability, not conditional-on-calibration, universal, distribution-shift-robust, or a hard physiological velocity bound.

## Frozen score and label contract

For each **independently acquired and blinded-reference-labeled** participant i, compute a finite exclusion-aggressiveness score `a_ij` for every eligible ordinary-camera gap j from a **fixed precommitted** E002-371 model and physically calibrated shutter/clock/geometry nuisance envelope; larger means more aggressive exclusion. The score is not allowed to be selected or tuned on the holdout reference labels. Let `H_ij` be independently verified hidden anatomical closure (1), verified absence (0), or unresolved/missing (unknown). Define participant nonconformity `S_i=max{a_ij: H_ij=1}`, with max of empty set `-infinity`; **any** unresolved/missing eligible label or unverified instrument assumption sets `S_i=+infinity`. A calibration participant must be an independent unit; never count 50 windows as 50 participants. For n participant scores, k as above, set q to the kth smallest S_i, or `+infinity` if k>n. On a new participant, the decision `EXCLUDE_CONDITIONALLY` is permitted only when `a_ij>q` **and** all physical evidence gates are independently satisfied; otherwise `ABSTAIN`. This rule is conditional on reliable reference truth in calibration, exchangeable participants, and a frozen score; it does not certify physiological absence.

The finite-sample rank claim is `P(any falsely excluded closure on a new participant) <= alpha` **marginally** over exchangeable calibration and test participants, with conservative treatment of ties. It is NOT a post-data, participant-specific guarantee, nor a substitute for physical shutter or human validation. A participant with an unknown reference window cannot be treated as having zero closures.

## Frozen falsification simulations

Use a deterministic, published pseudorandom seed and >=10,000 Monte Carlo trials per setting; report binomial Monte Carlo SE. Generate 1D continuous participant scores from independent standard normal draws, where an event is `S_test > q`. Test n=12, 19, 39 at alpha=.05. Prediction: n=12 gives q=+infinity and empirical false-exclusion risk exactly 0, with no finite exclusions; n=19 and n=39 exchangeable risks approximately 1/(n+1) and 2/(n+1), respectively (0.05 each), within 5 Monte Carlo standard errors of exact values. For shifted new participants `S_test ~ N(1.5,1)` with unchanged N(0,1) calibration, predict empirical risk >0.10 at n=19 and n=39, falsifying transport without exchangeability.

Second attack: hierarchical windows with n=12 participants, 50 windows each, latent participant mean N(0,1), within-participant noise N(0,0.1); naive iid-window q uses the order statistic at alpha=.05 over 600 windows, and an unseen participant is counted failed if **any** of 50 test windows exceeds q. Predict empirical new-participant familywise risk >0.05; report it separately from the valid participant-level n=12 abstention. This is a counterexample to using window count as effective participant sample size, not a claim about blink physiology.

## Direct study amendment and product gate

A 12/12 calibration/holdout split cannot support a nontrivial 95% participant-level distribution-free exclusion rule. **Do not silently claim that the planned 24-person pilot does.** For prospective 95% marginal risk calibration, require at least 19 calibration participants (plus disjoint holdouts) and recognize the near-max conservatism; larger calibration is preferable. This only matters *after* physically authenticated camera exposure and independently rated anatomical truth exist. Unseen gaze/glasses/lighting strata and nonexchangeable devices require explicit abstention or separately validated transport. All current Lucent outputs remain `ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED`; production `BLOCKED`.

## Kill criteria

Fail if iid simulations violate the exact rank expectation by >5 Monte Carlo SE, if the shifted or pseudoreplicated settings do not demonstrate the predicted >0.10 and >0.05 failures, if any unknown label is converted to absence, if n=12 yields a finite threshold, or if the report promotes marginal synthetic calibration into human or physical validation. Preserve failures rather than retune the seed or generator.
