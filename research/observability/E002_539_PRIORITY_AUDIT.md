# E002-539 primary-literature priority audit (2026-10-30)

**Question:** Is the speed-plus-acceleration saturation structure in E002-539 new mathematics, and does the application establish a new human physiological measurement?

**Answer:** No priority claim for the mathematics, and no human measurement. E002-539 uses the classical idea of optimal trajectories under simultaneous derivative/actuation constraints. The following primary works predate Lucent by decades:

1. Shin, K. G. and McKay, N. D. (1985), *Minimum-Time Control of Robotic Manipulators with Geometric Path Constraints*, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control 30(6), 531–541. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.1985.1104009. Established phase-plane constrained optimal control for robotic motion.
2. Bobrow, J. E., Dubowsky, S. and Gibson, J. S. (1985), *Time-Optimal Control of Robotic Manipulators Along Specified Paths*, International Journal of Robotics Research 4(3), 3–17. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/027836498500400301. Established constrained trajectory optimization with actuator bounds.
3. Sahar, G. and Hollerbach, J. M. (1986), *Planning of Minimum-Time Trajectories for Robot Arms*, International Journal of Robotics Research 5(3), 90–100. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/027836498600500305. Developed motion planning under dynamic and actuator constraints.
4. Existing E002-536 literature audit already documents Kwon et al.'s 600-fps human eyelid kinematics and Godfrey et al.'s 240-fps smartphone measurements; neither supplies the **hard continuous-time human speed and acceleration ceilings** E002-539 requires. See [E002_536_RESULT.md](E002_536_RESULT.md).

These works are **contextual prior art, not a proof that they state E002-539's exact aperture inequality**. The inequality has an elementary self-contained proof in [E002_539_RESULT.md](E002_539_RESULT.md); the nontrivial measurement-science question is whether independently calibrated real camera and physiological assumptions make that conditional inequality useful. E002-539's synthetic LP agreement does not answer that question.

**Novelty posture:** At most a specific observability/abstention consequence of classical bounded-motion control. A publishable world-class contribution still requires physically authenticated shutter timing, independently constrained human motion, prospective unseen-condition predictions and adversarial nuisance survival. Until then, no physiological priority, validated detection claim, or production release.
