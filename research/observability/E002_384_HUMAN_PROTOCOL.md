# E002-384 prospective human-validation protocol (NOT PERFORMED)

Aim: independently verify RGB-to-aperture error calibration at whole-recording level and the E002-371 shutter-gap certificate.

Consent and appropriate local oversight. Pair ordinary 30-fps RGB with independently acquired >=240-fps high-speed eyelid reference (preferably >=600 fps). Measure true exposure start/end, jitter and clock offsets with common LED/flash sync. Do not infer these from nominal FPS. Obtain physical aperture reference and uncertainty intervals, open/closed/intermediate calibration, and independently justified speed/acceleration bounds; otherwise abstain.

Target >=30 participants, 20 calibration and 10 untouched participant holdouts, plus a distinct unseen ordinary-RGB device holdout. Freeze estimator, optical error bound, gap law and abstention rules before evaluating holdouts. Record controlled illumination, low light, gaze, glasses, blur and partial occlusion. No sleep-deprivation induction.

Primary endpoint: all-60-frame optical residual coverage on untouched clips, with participant-cluster uncertainty. Secondary: false no-hidden-closure decisions per eligible gap and per participant, timing uncertainty, device shift and abstention. Explicitly label ambiguous reference frames as unknown. Report failures and negative findings. Any false hard certificate under declared conditions blocks product release. Zero observed errors is not proof of zero risk (rough upper bound 3/N for independent units).

Current status: prospective design only, not a claim of human validation.
