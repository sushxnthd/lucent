# E002-598 | Executable human and physical validation study handoff

**Status:** protocol only, no participants enrolled or human ratings collected. Development-only LPW review and new physical-camera study are distinct experiments. Obtain institutional ethics/privacy review and informed consent before collecting new human footage.

## Stage A: existing authenticated LPW footage, no physical shutter claims

1. Authorized study custodian privately reconstructs E002-578 native packets and E002-595 nominal-30-fps phase packets from the fixed authenticated development source. Preserve owner-only frame mappings separately; no public video/image/annotation artifacts.
2. Two independent raters receive separate blinded `RATER_A` and `RATER_B` native packets, and separately randomized phase-arm packets. Raters must not see the other rater's judgments, participant/source identifiers, frame-index mappings or E002-371 predictions.
3. Each reviewer opens `e002_598_offline_rater.html` locally and selects the assigned native E002-574 template (190 images) or an E002-595 ARM template (60 images). Mark the same iris-centered x column, upper/lower lid margins and conservative pixel uncertainty. Flag occlusion, blur, gaze, glasses, geometry, brightness or missingness instead of forcing a coordinate. Export JSON privately.
4. The custodian independently establishes iris-diameter scale and uncertainty from frames where anatomy is visible, with documented geometry; never copy an unverified iris scale from the same closed-eye frame. For the ten-clip development packets, use E002-599's native-review bridge with owner-private, independently justified iris scale, registered x column and local eyelid-slope uncertainty. Do **not** feed E002-598 exports directly to E002-576: that evaluator expects a different six-key row schema and 330 held-out clips. E002-576 remains a separate held-out evaluator and requires an explicitly verified, non-leaking schema adapter before future use. Both pipelines must abstain on missing/discordant intervals, nuisance flags, unverified scale/slope or missing frames. Seal native-rate adjudication before opening phase-arm ratings.
5. Primary development outcome: number of source-bound native-frame closure **witnesses** with open decimated endpoints by predefined phase, plus coverage/abstention and rater disagreement. Do not interpret this as physical 30-fps camera performance or continuous-time truth. Report per-window and per-participant counts, not a binomial confidence interval over temporally correlated frames. Participant 1 is development only.

## Stage B: independent synchronized physical camera study

**Acquisition.** Recruit a consented adult exploratory pilot (target 20 people, with participant-disjoint calibration and held-out groups; this target is operational, not a power calculation). Use a borrowed or already available high-speed reference camera, preferably 240 fps or higher, and a real 30-fps ordinary camera simultaneously viewing the same eye. Record natural and instructed blinks in separate strata, varied gaze, lighting and glasses; record missed frames, motion blur and occlusion. Use fixed focus/exposure where possible. Do not conflate instructed blink kinetics with spontaneous behavior.

**Independent optical calibration.** Before human acquisition, measure each camera's exposure start/end, exposure-response curve, rolling-row timing and clock alignment with an externally timed LED/photodiode or equivalent instrument, plus a geometric eye-plane target. Keep calibration uncertainty intervals and raw instrument provenance. A nominal FPS or container PTS is insufficient. If such equipment is unavailable, stop at Stage A and retain ABSTAIN; no substitute software metadata is physical calibration.

**Anatomical reference.** Two blinded independent raters mark high-speed upper/lower eyelid margins with pixel uncertainty and explicit nuisance flags. A reference-confirmed closure requires conservative interval adjudication and adequate temporal support. An observed high-rate frame is itself an integrated sample, not a continuous-time oracle. An ambiguous event is UNCERTAIN, never silently OPEN.

**Motion premise.** A hard continuous-time velocity or acceleration ceiling cannot be inferred from the maximum sampled frame-to-frame speed. Require a separately justified physical envelope with documented transfer limits; otherwise all deterministic E002-371 exclusions ABSTAIN. An empirically fitted percentile can support only explicitly probabilistic, condition-specific calibration, not a universal hard certificate.

**Prospective prediction file.** Before unblinding held-out reference annotations, seal for each ordinary-camera unexposed gap: participant/session pseudonym, source/clock/exposure-calibration digest, gap time interval and upper duration g, endpoint lower aperture bounds l0,l1, closure threshold tau, independently certified V and its source, nuisance/visibility/geometry flags, and the predeclared strict decision `V*g < (l0-tau)+(l1-tau)`. Store the file with timestamped hash in private custody. If any required physical premise is missing, predict ABSTAIN, not exclusion.

**Primary falsification.** Any independently adjudicated reference closure in a gap predicted `EXCLUDE_HIDDEN_CLOSURE` is a violation of the combined physical certificate and requires identifying which premise failed. Primary report: number of eligible and abstained gaps, number of exclusions, violations among exclusions, uncertainty/geometry/brightness/blur/gaze/glasses/missingness/clock strata, and per-participant coverage. Preserve all negative outcomes. Do not use same-subject frames as independent statistical replicates; report participant-level uncertainty and clustered sensitivity analyses. Twenty pilot subjects cannot establish a rare-event safety guarantee.

**Adversarial holdout.** Freeze all calibration and decision code on the development group. Hold out participants, camera exposure settings, gaze/eyewear conditions and lighting combinations. Include deliberately injected clock offsets, rolling-shutter miscalibration, frame loss, exposure response nonlinearity and occlusion in *separate labeled* adversarial trials. The robust decision must abstain when uncertainty exceeds the authenticated budget.

## Stop and release rules

- **Stop deterministic certification** if independent physical velocity/acceleration premises cannot be defended, if any gap timing/geometry/radiometric bound is uncalibrated, or if a held-out exclusion is contradicted by an adjudicated closure.
- **Kill a candidate observable** if nuisance variation overwhelms anatomical signal or if its uncertainty cannot be bounded on unseen conditions. E002-597 already rejected uncalibrated dark-pixel and vertical-edge proxies.
- **No medical, sleepiness or no-hidden-blink claims** from these measurements without separate construct validity, safety review and external replication.
- Release remains `BLOCKED`; operational product output `ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED`. External physical and ethics review is required before promotion.

**Data custody:** minimize raw eye/face video, encrypt private storage, separate reviewer identity from subject mapping, obtain consent for any sharing, delete private reviewer packets according to an approved retention plan, and commit only deidentified aggregate evidence and reproduction code. No automated GitHub job should collect or publish private human ratings.
