## New operational result

E002-472 specified a real optical-phase calibration but did not provide a machine-checkable path from the two independent data streams to an auditable intake decision. E002-477 implements that missing measurement interface. The standard-library [validator](e002_477_optical_intake.py) consumes a manifest, camera-response CSV, **separately recorded** photodiode/logic-analyzer pulse-edge CSV, and raw-frame bundle. It verifies all three source hashes, phase/repeat/row completeness, measured pulse width and phase, gain lock, dropped-frame/clipping flags and declared clock uncertainty. A checksum is an integrity check **not** an attestation of actual hardware independence. An independent reviewer must inspect the acquisition chain and raw trace.

The synthetic fixture models two camera rows with one-millisecond rectangular shutter supports beginning at **0.20 ms and 0.35 ms**. It simulates a 240-fps frame period, 0.05-ms optical phase increments across two frame periods, a 0.10-ms optical pulse and 20 repeats per phase. This yields **168 phase bins × 20 repeats × 2 rows = 6,720** paired camera/clock observations. The validator accepts the complete fixture as `PASS_INTAKE_ONLY`, with descriptive response-weighted phase centroids **0.65 and 0.80 ms**, preserving the planted **0.15-ms row difference**. These centroids are descriptive and **not** independently authenticated shutter edges or an exposure-support certificate.

## Scientific interpretation and limits

This is a **physical-acquisition readiness artifact**, not a new observability theorem, a validated physical shutter, or human evidence. It demonstrates that a two-source optical phase-scan bundle can be ingested without silently accepting prespecified completeness, timing and integrity failures. It does **not** show that any real camera meets the declared tolerances. The separate clock stream may itself be fabricated or incorrectly synchronized; software declarations cannot authenticate hardware. The validator deliberately does not infer zero exposure from a nondetected optical pulse (E002-466), infer actual rolling-shutter support, certify pulse energy, validate sensor linearity/noise tails, or estimate a physiological velocity cap.

The physical gate is now actionable rather than only prose: borrow an independently timed optical pulse/photodiode and a RAW-capable camera; record two or more sensor-row strata with locked gain; retain full source files and independently measured clock transfer; run this intake; then independently calibrate detector dead zones, row kernels, timing and held-out optical predictions before using E002-371. Any intake failure is a rejected configuration, not a no-closure observation. Only after physical encoder-grounded moving-aperture validation should consented, blinded participant-disjoint human acquisition proceed. See [acquisition contract](E002_477_ACQUISITION.md) and [E002-472 handoff](E002_472_PHYSICAL_HANDOFF.md).
