## What changed scientifically
E002-463–466 repeatedly strengthened synthetic reference-instrument assumptions without acquiring physical data. This run instead identifies **two concrete independent human acquisition routes** and freezes a reproducible holdout before seeing their recordings.

**Public route:** Guttmann-Flury, Sheng & Zhu, *Scientific Data* 12:587 (2025), DOI [10.1038/s41597-025-04861-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04861-9), dataset [Synapse syn64005218](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn64005218/datasets/) / DOI 10.7303/syn64005218. The authors describe **31 people / 63 sessions**, 150-fps Phantom Miro M310 monocular eye video (320x240), 300-Hz Tobii eye tracking, 1-kHz EEG/EOG/EMG and aligned blink markers. The data descriptor identifies video synchronization failures for P3004L101 and ME181, late-start recordings and other missingness. The video rate is **150 fps, not 300 fps**. CC0 is declared for the original dataset. It offers independently collected *human image-level* challenge data, not authenticated physical shutter integration or complete continuous anatomy. The 2026 NEMAR/Zenodo EEG rehosts must not be mistaken for video data.

**Higher-rate restricted route:** Clites, Kim & Rootman, [FaceBase DOI 10.25550/88-28BJ](https://doi.org/10.25550/88-28BJ), released September 2026: **eight adults**, 400-fps 3D eyelid-margin markers and synchronized orbicularis oculi EMG across five behaviors. Source: [FaceBase release](https://trackhub.facebase.org/2026/09/01/recent-release-clites-eyelid-kinematics-emg/); associated [PNAS physiology paper](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2508058122). Individual human data require approved controlled access, General Research Use / Not-for-profit only; the published FaceBase FAQ describes signed forms and approximately 4–6 weeks of review. No approval or download has been obtained. 400-fps sampled markers are not continuous anatomical ground truth either.

**Relevant nuisance priority:** [2026 vertical-gaze confounding study](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-026-02984-4) strengthens the need for gaze-stratified tests; it does not refute or validate the E002-459 held-out velocity analysis.

## Frozen prospective test
[E002-467 protocol](E002_467_FROZEN.md) was committed **before** the code or any dataset examination (commit `80f2e6ec40abb5e3ec12ceb10ffb4182734ab374`). For Eye-BCI, 23 of 31 subjects are assigned to calibration and eight to holdout by fixed SHA-256 order; all sessions remain subject-disjoint. Image candidates require strict uncertainty-separated subthreshold frames and passing declared nuisance/annotation/synchrony flags. A frame above threshold is **UNRESOLVED**, never anatomical negative ground truth. The candidate output is not a physical crossing witness.

The [executable preflight](e002_467_human_video_preflight.py) accepts annotated per-frame CSVs with participant, sequence, monotonically increasing frame index and timestamps, aperture/error, and explicit quality, annotation, geometry, sync, shutter and nuisance flags. It refuses malformed/missing fields, skips/duplicate frames, timing discontinuities, nonfinite/negative uncertainty and unverified flags. Public output is aggregate by split, without participant IDs, raw timestamps or images. All physical claims remain `ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED`. [Adversarial tests](test_e002_467_human_video_preflight.py) and [hosted workflow](../../.github/workflows/e002_467_human_video_preflight.yml) exercise the non-release contract.

**Important scope limit:** Flags in an imported CSV are declarations, not independent proof of calibration. No external video was acquired, manually annotated or run through this tool during this turn. The tool tests are synthetic unit tests, not held-out human results. No statistical coverage or error rate is estimated. Hosted CI run [40362133685](https://github.com/sushxnthd/lucent/actions/runs/40362133685) was queued at writing; success is not claimed.
