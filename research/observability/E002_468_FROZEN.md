## Frozen measurement and success/failure gates
1. Attempt a bounded HTTPS download in ephemeral CI, verify the published archive MD5 before reading any contents, and record SHA-256. If the download, hash or ZIP integrity fails, report **ACQUISITION_FAILED**; do not substitute fabricated measurements.
2. Extract only the listed video/XML/sync files in an ephemeral runner, enforcing size and safe-path limits. Compute SHA-256 of each. Never upload raw human images, video, gaze or per-frame records.
3. Use ffprobe to record AVI coded dimensions, codec, rational r_frame_rate and avg_frame_rate, nb_frames and duration, plus first 120 decoded-frame presentation timestamps. Calculate median/min/max timestamp step and counts of nonmonotonic/duplicate/missing timestamps. AVI timestamps may be generated by the container and **are not authenticated hardware exposure times**.
4. Independently decode at most the first 512 frames with ffmpeg to 64x48 gray. Report only aggregate frame-to-frame mean absolute intensity change (median/p95/max), mean brightness and number of decodable frames. These are empirical *video-signal diagnostics*, not eyelid aperture measurements or closure labels. Report any decode error and abstain.
5. Predeclared interpretation: if video container fps differs from 150 by >1%, flag **NOMINAL_RATE_CONFLICT**; also compare the rehost's ~500-fps statement without assuming either is authoritative. Even if they agree, the sensor's physical acquisition rate and shutter support remain **UNAUTHENTICATED**.
6. The public output is a nonidentifying aggregate JSON and a scientific report. No participant-level anatomical metrics, medical claims, or release of physical hidden-closure exclusion. Keep E002-371 ABSTAIN_UNCALIBRATED until independent shutter/geometry/velocity reference exists.
