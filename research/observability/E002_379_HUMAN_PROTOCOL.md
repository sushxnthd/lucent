Concurrent 30/60fps ordinary RGB and >=600fps eye-reference with measured native exposure timestamps and two independent clock anchors. At least 30 spontaneous and 30 voluntary blinks each under bright/dim lighting, gaze, glasses, blur and motion. Freeze all thresholds, reference error/acceleration assumptions, split and exclusion rules before holdout. Estimate reference speed with bounded aperture error and timing uncertainty, comparing adjacent and multiscale secants. Predict no-hidden-closure margin on each holdout gap before inspecting highspeed labels. Falsify upon any closure in a certified interval when assumptions are independently verified. Compare geometry-only, brightness-only, missingness-only and smoothness-only controls; report per-participant and per-device coverage/abstention, not frame bootstrap. Crucial: finite reference samples cannot certify a universal acceleration bound; obtain independent biomechanical bounds or downgrade to statistical prediction. No clinical fatigue claims.
