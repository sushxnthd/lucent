# E002-472 — Physical acquisition and blinded human-validation handoff
Date: 2026-10-27. **Protocol only: no physical instrument or new anatomical human measurements were accessed.** This supplements, not replaces, E002-456/461/463/464/465/466/467/471.

## Instrument design choice before acquisition
The frozen design matrix rejects a 30-fps, 4-ms exposure, 1-mm aperture-margin configuration for an illustrative 100-mm/s cap (critical speed 50.66 mm/s). At 240 fps, 4-ms exposure and 0.1-ms clock error, no guaranteed dark gap exists at all. Prefer the **240-fps / 1-ms exposure** configuration for a pilot **only if** independently authenticated camera shutter and a 0.2-ms reference integration with <=0.1-ms error on each clock are achievable. At the illustrative margin/uncertainty, its conditional critical speed is 369.86 mm/s; this does **not** establish that any human velocity bound exists.

## A. Independent shutter and reference calibration (no human subjects)
1. Use a borrowed camera capable of raw frame access, manually locked exposure and gain, a borrowed pulse source/photodiode or oscilloscope, and a stable optical pulse imaged **through the same sensor path**. A phone's advertised slow-motion fps or LED trigger timing is not an exposure certificate. If no independent timing instrument can be borrowed, **stop at protocol-only evidence**.
2. Scan a short pulse over at least two complete frame periods with phase increments no coarser than 0.05 ms; repeat >=20 times per phase and repeat after warm-up. Record raw response, dark/flat controls, measured pulse width, photodiode sensitivity floor, latency and uncertainty. Characterize nonzero tails, automatic gain, rolling-row differences and dropped frames. No thresholded non-detection may be treated as zero exposure without the E002-466 dead-zone bound.
3. Infer a conservative interval for actual shutter start/end **from imaging-path response**, not from trigger edges. Preserve per-row timing when rolling shutter is present; if a single global shutter interval is not justified, abstain rather than pooling rows.
4. Verify the reference sensor's dynamic delay, response support, and nonnegative weighting with an independently measured moving aperture. Ringing or unknown filter support invalidates an anatomical positive witness (E002-464). Authenticate cross-camera time mapping and drift with a shared independently timestamped optical signal.
5. Calibration acceptance for the *illustrative 240-fps/1-ms design*: total ordinary-camera clock bound <=0.1 ms; reference clock bound <=0.1 ms; reference exposure <=0.2 ms; ordinary exposure <=1.0 ms; no frame losses in accepted intervals; aperture measurement uncertainty <=0.1 mm after geometric calibration. **These are prespecified target tolerances, not achieved specifications.** Record every rejected run and failed target.

## B. Moving-aperture falsification before human interpretation
- Use an independently measured physical aperture traversing above/below a fixed threshold at multiple speeds, including just-inside and just-outside the guaranteed dark-gap boundary. Do not use the ordinary camera's own frame stream to label physical truth.
- Acquire paired ordinary-camera and reference streams; freeze shutter intervals, encoder transfer-function bounds and reference-support uncertainty **before** running the hidden-closure decision.
- Inject geometry shift, gain/brightness drift, missing frames, motion blur, row skew, clock offset/drift, gaze-like affine deformation and glasses-like reflections. If any nuisance is unresolved, log `ABSTAIN`; never silently substitute an idealized correction.
- Each excluded interval with a valid independently witnessed crossing is a falsification of the joint calibration/model/cap assumptions. Do not silently delete such intervals. A clean synthetic bench cannot establish human physiological bounds.

## C. Blinded human study
- Obtain appropriate consent and institutional review before collecting identifiable eye videos. Keep raw data off public GitHub; publish code, protocol, deidentified aggregate counts and reproducible checksums where permitted.
- Freeze a participant-disjoint 23-calibration / 8-holdout allocation for a 31-participant study, or label a smaller collection explicitly as a pilot, not confirmatory validation. Collect spontaneous and voluntary blinks in neutral/up/down gaze, varying brightness, head movement and glasses/no-glasses. Record missingness and exposure metadata prospectively.
- Two independent blinded raters annotate reference eyelid margins in calibrated millimeters with uncertainty and repeat sheets. A positive reference must have its **entire exposure and timing uncertainty** inside the guaranteed ordinary dark gap. Open-looking frames and lack of positive witnesses are never anatomical negatives.
- Estimate descriptive velocity distributions with participant clustering. Do not infer a universal hard physiological speed cap from sample maxima. For an externally justified cap, lock it and the nuisance model before opening holdout; otherwise report only **probabilistic** calibration and coverage, with abstention, and prohibit deterministic no-closure claims.
- Report by participant and nuisance stratum: total candidate intervals, abstentions, conditional exclusions, independently witnessed counterexamples, reference-indeterminate cases, timing/calibration failures, and confidence intervals based on participant units. Preserve every counterexample and negative result.

## Release blocker checklist
All must be independently reviewed and evidenced before **any** physical hidden-closure exclusion claim: actual shutter support and detector dead-zone; clock/row timing; mm-scale geometry; encoder delay and filter support; independent positive reference; nuisance failure injection; preregistered unseen-participant evaluation; externally defensible velocity assumption; claim calibration and abstention; privacy/consent and reproducibility. None is currently satisfied by E002-472 alone. Blink duration, PERCLOS, fatigue and medical inference require additional validation and remain blocked even if the optical experiment succeeds.

**Stop rule:** If independent physical timing or geometry cannot be obtained, do not create additional nominal-FPS datasets or speculative synthetic physiological bounds. Preserve this protocol and seek physical access or an authenticated public acquisition.
