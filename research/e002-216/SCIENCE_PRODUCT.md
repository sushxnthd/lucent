# E002-216 | EyeDentify native-time label identifiability audit

## One active measurement kernel
**Signal/reference:** Ordinary-RGB anatomical pupil/iris waveform against native-time same-eye tracker pupil diameter (mm).
**Frontier:** Existing EyeDentify three-second image/label data and pooled PupilSense results do not certify native-time five-second waveforms.
**Scalar metric:** Held-out participant-bootstrap lower Pearson correlation bound; separate frozen lag, amplitude, NRMSE and 75% coverage gates.
**Strongest confound:** Reference temporal preprocessing and clock alignment, then focus/contrast/scale/gaze/eyelid effects.
**Decisive falsification:** Untouched, synchronized participant/device/lighting/glasses holdout waveforms fail frozen metrics or nuisance controls.
**Publishable success:** >=8 untouched participants, independent checker, prior-art audit, unseen boundary prediction and meaningful holdouts.
**Product threshold:** >=75% prospective participant acceptance, with calibration/abstention that works without post-hoc eye selection.
**Kill condition:** Three nondiscriminating primary runs, prospective failure, or two blocked accesses to a source path.

## SCIENCE: exact conditional failure
Published EyeDentify alignment implementation at Git blob f46d7ae369a62596f7044ca647d361c38fcb16ea converts timestamps to whole seconds, separates the first three second-groups, rowwise-averages the corresponding pupil columns, then pairs resulting rows to webcam frames by index. It also repeats one timestamp across output rows. The project documentation explicitly describes three-second recordings, webcam at ~30 fps, tracker at ~90 Hz, and the row-wise mean.

For three equal 90-sample tracker blocks, the operator is y[j]=(x[j]+x[90+j]+x[180+j])/3 for j=0..89. Its rank is 90 and nullity 180, so infinitely many distinct true pupil waveforms yield identical processed labels. For a sinusoid of frequency f Hz, H(f)=(1+exp(2*pi*i*f)+exp(4*pi*i*f))/3. At 1/3 Hz, all true variation cancels. At 1 Hz, a perfectly measured 30-fps RGB sine has correlation 0 with the index-paired folded labels (synthetic). For ramp x(t)=t, label[j]=1+j/90 but RGB frame[j] samples j/30; mismatch 1-j/45 seconds, from +1.000 to -0.978 s, RMS ~0.577 s. These are conditional exact/synthetic findings, NOT human data.

**Limitations:** We have not inspected distributed Kaggle files or proven which code revision produced them. Unequal per-second sample counts and missing values can change the transform. The result does not imply RGB itself cannot recover pupil dynamics. The rank/nullspace is elementary linear algebra, not a novel general theorem.

## PRODUCT: capability consequence
Do NOT use output labels from this fold for native-time waveform correlation, lag, amplitude, NRMSE, or five-second certification. Recover original Tobii millisecond records and actual video frame timestamps, align by time, and validate reference quality before measuring RGB. If raw records cannot be recovered, switch dataset. No fatigue/state inference is justified.
