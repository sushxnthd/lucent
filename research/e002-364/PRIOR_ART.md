# Primary prior-art audit (2026-10-22)
Shuffled rolling shutter for snapshot temporal imaging, Optics Express 2022, DOI 10.1364/OE.444864, already models shutter-integrated spatiotemporal sampling and temporal recovery.
Compressive temporal imaging using a rolling shutter camera array, PMID 33985028 (2021), demonstrates rolling-shutter temporal coding.
Navascues-Cornago et al., Video frame rate influences the accuracy of blink dynamics measurement, Ocular Surface 40:22-30 (2026), DOI 10.1016/j.jtos.2026.01.003, evaluates 500-fps human reference and frame-rate sensitivity.
Culemann et al., Blink parameters are confounded by vertical eye orientation, Behavior Research Methods 58 (2026), DOI 10.3758/s13428-026-02984-4, shows gaze confounds for pupil and eyelid methods.
High-speed camera characterization of voluntary eye blinking kinematics, DOI 10.1098/rsif.2013.0227, establishes complex physiological blink kinematics.
BlinkNet-Q, PMID 42684105 (2026), establishes camera blink detection as prior art.
Priority verdict: exact reduced-model bounds are not yet a publishable new human observability law; no independent human evaluation completed.
