# E002-155 — FROZEN original-human visible-eye aperture drift audit (2026-10-13)
Classification: DISCRIMINATION and CAPABILITY-BUILDING.
Active kernel: can ordinary RGB preserve pupil waveform relative to synchronized tracker under nuisance shifts? This run tests one necessary intermediate condition, visible-eye denominator stability, NOT tracker waveform.
Signal/reference: public original-human 640x480 RGB-associated annotated pupil (red >=128) and visible-eye (red OR green >=128) masks, Zenodo record 20492528. No anatomical iris annotation, synchronized tracker, or state labels.
Pinned archive SHA256: 049df72454682c1c77c50c8236bb607919a6e333528bc2b89c567b625e459700. MD5: a30eafe5e71cef9de1e08b91988f88b6. License CC-BY-4.0. Do not redistribute images.
Eligibility: PNG annotations under annotation/png; filename pattern PREFIX_frame_NUMBER_pic_NUMBER; >=16 pupil pixels, >=64 eye pixels, 0<sqrt(P/E)<0.9, eye width >=8. Unique frame number within exact filename prefix; duplicates excluded. Group labels are not verified participant identifiers. Pair only exact frame-index differences k in {1,5,15,30}. No assumed FPS.
Observable: pupil area P, visible-eye area E, eye-mask bbox width W, D=E/W^2, Pn=P/W^2, q=sqrt(P/E). For each pair dD=log(D2/D1), dE=log(E2/E1), dPn=log(Pn2/Pn1), dq=log(q2/q1). Identity dq=(dPn-dD)/2. Scalar aperture confound A_k = Pr(|dD|>T), T=-2 log(0.95). A_k is the proportion of pairs where denominator drift alone, if width-normalized pupil area were fixed, could generate an apparent >5% pupil-radius response. This is NOT physiological error.
Frozen gates: G0 >=100 k=1 pairs and >=10 filename groups; G1 A_1<=0.05; G2 >=100 k=30 pairs and A_30<=0.05; G3 group-bootstrap (B=2000, seed=1552026) upper 95% percentile for A_1<=0.05; G4 max decomposition residual<=1e-10; G5 tracker waveform, RGB segmenter, full iris and fatigue all UNTESTED.
Baselines: unnormalized visible-eye area E; subgroup reports for 'eye' vs 'camera' prefixes and aperture opening/closing. Wildcard (~10%): 10 extreme adjacent pairs. Do not change gates based on results.
Mechanistic prediction: eyelid aperture/pose can change visible-eye denominator even with perfect masks; scale normalization by W^2 may remove global scale but not eyelid effects. Strongest confounds: W itself depends on gaze/crop, unknown acquisition timing, segmentation annotation error, photometric shifts untested.
Decisive falsification: A_k>0.05 or cluster uncertainty exceeds tolerance invalidates unconditional visible-eye-area reference for that lag, not anatomical-iris or true RGB pupil observability. Publishable success requires frozen participant/device held-out RGB vs synchronized tracker waveform metrics and confound controls; product-relevant success requires an independently validated abstention gate.
