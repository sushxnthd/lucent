# Primary prior-art checkpoint — 2026-10-20
- Soukupová and Čech, Real-Time Eye Blink Detection using Facial Landmarks (2016): https://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/ftp/articles/cech/Soukupova-TR-2016-05.pdf . Standard-camera EAR already established.
- Al-gawwam and Benaissa, Robust Eye Blink Detection Based on Eye Landmarks and Savitzky–Golay Filtering (2018): https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/9/4/93 . Vertical lid separation already established.
- Pedone, Flusser and Heikkilä, Blur invariant translational image registration for N-fold symmetric blurs (2013): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23782811/ . Blur-invariant registration already established.
- Wolter et al., Blinking Beyond EAR: A Stable Eyelid Angle Metric for Driver Drowsiness Detection and Data Augmentation (2025 preprint): https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19519 . 3D pose-aware eyelid geometry.
- Convolution first moments and bounded-likelihood-ratio total-variation inequalities are classical.
